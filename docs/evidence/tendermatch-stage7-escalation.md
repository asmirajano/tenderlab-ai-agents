## Durable queue and result boundary

Seven tables: escalation_plan, escalation_decision, escalation_request,
escalation_authorization, escalation_job, escalation_artifact, escalation_event.
Request intent, execution authority, queue status and advisory artifact are separate.
Plan/decision/request/artifact/authorization/event history is immutable; only bounded
job state transitions are mutable. Writer cannot issue its own grant or event.

One dispatch is atomically reserved under a live lease and grant. Duplicate/stale
dispatchers cannot call. An undispatched expired lease may be reclaimed within
three attempts; a dispatched unknown outcome is terminal and not automatically
retried. Only confirmed retryable failures enter bounded backoff. Provider idempotency
and cancellation/deadline enforcement remain requirements for a future adapter.

Output requires seven explicit Full TORS sections, supported/missing/conflicting
states, exact supplied evidence IDs, limitations and advisory/human-review authority.
No numeric AI score field is permitted. Validation and persistence are separate;
SQL independently validates shape/references and requires atomic artifact+success.
Reference membership is not proof of semantic correctness. No real artifact exists.
